Talent is everywhere.
Opportunity isn't.

Every child is born with curiosity, imagination and the ability to learn. But access to education is not always equal.

A child may have the desire to learn but not enough books. A student may want to explore technology but never have access to a computer. Another may need someone to guide them, encourage them and simply tell them, “You can do this.”

EDU works to bridge these gaps by bringing learning resources, digital opportunities, mentorship and community support closer to children who need them most.

Our Mission

Education should open doors, not create barriers.

Our mission is simple: to help children access meaningful learning opportunities regardless of where they come from or what circumstances they face.

EDU partners with communities to support children through educational resources, digital learning, mentorship and skill development.

We believe lasting change happens when children are not simply given opportunities, but are supported to make the most of them.

What EDU Does

How we help children move forward.

  1. 01

    Learn

    Strengthening the foundations of learning.

    We support children with age-appropriate learning resources, reading activities and additional academic support that help make learning easier and more engaging.

  2. 02

    Access

    Bringing learning resources closer.

    Books, educational materials and digital tools can make a meaningful difference when they reach children who need them.

  3. 03

    Connect

    Learning through people and technology.

    We create opportunities for children to explore digital learning, discover new interests and connect with mentors who can guide them.

  4. 04

    Grow

    Building confidence beyond the classroom.

    Education is more than marks. We help children develop curiosity, communication, confidence and the skills to imagine a future of their own.

Stories of Learning

Stories of learning and possibility.

Every child has a story. These are stories of children, teachers, volunteers and communities showing what becomes possible when learning gets the support it needs.

UNICEF India

When a classroom finally speaks your language

Savitri's story — Odisha

Savitri Pingwa, a young girl from a tribal community in Odisha, found classroom learning difficult when lessons were not in a language she fully understood. Mother-tongue-based learning helped her participate more confidently and reconnect learning with the world she knew.

UNICEF India

The mentors who made learning fun

Prerana Mitras — Andhra Pradesh

University students volunteered as mentors in government schools, using storytelling, games, puzzles, arts and crafts to make foundational learning more engaging. UNICEF reports that more than 4,000 students from 19 universities took part in the programme's first year.

Read the story →

UNICEF India

When girls teach each other to belong

Kasturba Gandhi Balika Vidyalaya — Bihar

In Purnea, teachers and students worked to make learning more inclusive for deaf girls. Teachers learned Indian Sign Language, students supported one another, and peer learning became part of a more inclusive school environment.
